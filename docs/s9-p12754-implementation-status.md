## Safety and recovery
Offline arm requires fresh cached thermal witness BIG<=60 C LITTLE<=59 C GPU<=60 C battery<45 C. Offline WAIT also requires the same <=60/59/60 C headroom continuously for 20 s; measurement soft 67/66/68 C and hard 76/70/72/47 C cutoffs are preserved. WAIT union bits: 1 link, 2 FG freshness, 4 external status, 8 display, 16 GPU, 32 thermal, 64 load, 128 UFS busy, 256 no touch, 512 input quiet, 1024 conflict, 2048 wake reset. preflight uses stop-mask thermal bits. No wait after hard cutoff.
p12754_rate is a cached governor measurement, fresh <=3 s. Host retry only frozen row selector after missing echo, checking nonce, arm and phase identity; no automatic retry of arm or policy. Failed WAIT start=0 is no measurement. No physical CPU OFF, voltage or bus writes, DCS, MFC/ISPPRE gate tests.

## P127541 field evidence and intervention
The two logs 005408 and 010606 showed respectively stop 512 with CPU sum 361%, and WAIT timeout with seen 224 (thermal/CPU/UFS) and longest quiet 17.81 s. Revision 2 adds read-only p127541_stats: nBIT = blocker rising-edge count, tBIT = sampled time in that blocker in mach ticks (use status hz), skips = active skipped 200ms checks. WAIT union includes capture CPU/UFS pauses; quiet_ticks reports WAIT only. Host writes wait541.txt and UFS before/after bracket ufs541.json (includes WAIT/setup/recovery delay). No attribution of I/O origin from a worker or aggregate.

ABOX existing opt-in DMA stop/resume remains guarded by HAL hint, no queued frames/live route, acknowledged stop and retained ring. Resume metric now requires two normal-loop in-ring position samples with RDMA_PROGRESS and motion within 200 ms, not ENABLE alone; otherwise record fault, disable further idle attempts in that stream and keep existing refill/fallback. Functional playback/capture/headphone/route check and matched battery A/B/A remain required. No new boot-time policy. LITTLE stages remain unchanged; reports export the two configured BUCK3 uV samples, compare all B samples against both baseline windows, and retain actual measured-rate/restore gate.

For CPU owner ranking use existing ThermalTrace then ThermalTraceDump (separate, not during audit). Host-only s9-p127541-background.ps1 summarizes live fenced process samples, native UFS summaries and frozen PID/task/TID pages. It does not claim complete trace coverage or identify an originating UFS writer from temporal correlation.

Native firmware build and device qualification remain required. Source-contract and PowerShell tests are not hardware evidence.
