# P1278.1 (Rev8.1) S9 connected display recovery and foreground pilots

## Scope
Rev7 demonstrated same-boot automatic LITTLE 598 MHz and ABOX qualification, lease release on wake and stock restore. A real S9 showed a stationary black region at the left corner and a gray flash at wake from panel sleep; UI motion otherwise normal, reboot cleared the corruption. ## Real Rev8 log 060150 regression
Kernel 2a257042 booted; Auto Idle stage5 mode69 PASS and final stock restored. Panel never sent DCS28 or DCS10: actual DSC_CONTROL0 0xe022a has SLICE_MODE_CH=1 and DUAL_SLICE_EN=0, correct for two DSC encoders/two slices (Samsung decon_reg.c 1044–1073). Rev8 wrongly rejected bit3. DSIM_INTMSK 0x8e7b4447 bit24 unmasked, INTSRC 0x23040000 contains old FRAME_DONE; never clear that bit or assume ownership. Snapshot FIFO 0x4507 and PHY 0x40f are compatible with passive drain. C fixture reproduces the complete snapshot with negative mutations. Refusal bits: 1 capture, 2 link, 4 DSC/geometry, 8 FIFO, 16 hold, 32 trigger restoration, 64 changed configuration, 128 passive drain, 256 ID/eligibility, 512 command, 1024 frame. `first` now means submit frame counter, `done` means latched frame counter. Three `drain` samples required for host wake qualification. `refusal` clears only on clean wake. A nonzero raw IRQ flag does not certify transfer. Fix literal escaped newline in snapshots.
